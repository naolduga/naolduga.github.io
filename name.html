Name

January 27, 2026

I go by "Naol Duga Jebessa" or "ND Jebessa" in the West. Some friends call me ND, per the initials of my "first name". You see, my given name is Naol, and my father's name is Duga. To fit in the Western naming convention, I have resorted to using my grandfather's name (Jebessa) as a "last name" or family name.

Depending on the context, I am aka 'Naod', ቾ**, ጫ*, ቱ*, "ክንፈ ገብርኤል" etc. More on this in the future, perhaps.
